Alberta Self-Managed Condo Board Kit

For volunteer boards of small Alberta condominium corporations (4–30 units) that run without a management company. Since 1 April 2026 the Condominium Dispute Resolution Tribunal (CDRT) hears owner complaints about access to minutes, budgets and records, and from 4 November 2026 an owner pays a $150 fee to file one. This kit gives your board the dates and the paper trail before the first complaint lands.

  • 12-month dated compliance calendar built from your fiscal year-end and AGM date — budget, AGM notice, financial statements, insurance review, reserve-fund items — as .csv and .ics (imports into Google / Apple / Outlook).
  • Board minutes template and AGM notice template in plain Markdown/Word-compatible text.
  • Owner records-request log — log every document request and your response date.

Who this is not for

  • Corporations with a licensed condominium manager — your manager already keeps this calendar.
  • Boards looking for software for payments, amenity booking or resident portals — look at PayHOA, Kohabit or Condo Control.
  • Anyone who needs legal advice. This is an organizer, not advice: verify every date against the Condominium Property Act, the Condominium Property Regulation and your bylaws, and ask a lawyer when in doubt.
